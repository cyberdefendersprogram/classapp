The assignment

Research the 2013 Target data breach. Write a case study that maps the intrusion to MITRE ATT&CK, says what a network sensor would have seen (including the alerts that fired), and says what you would deploy today to stop or detect a similar attack. This is the “alerts were there, nobody acted” case: it connects directly to the signal-versus-noise work from Class 2 and to Bejtlich Ch. 1 (why monitoring is not enough without response).

Start here (then verify)

  • U.S. Senate Commerce Committee staff report, A “Kill Chain” Analysis of the 2013 Target Data Breach (March 2014). Primary-style source for the timeline.
  • Brian Krebs, Target Hackers Broke in Via HVAC Company (krebsonsecurity.com, February 2014).
  • Bloomberg Businessweek, Missed Alarms and 40 Million Stolen Credit Card Numbers (March 2014).
  • Target’s 2013 annual filing and the 2017 multistate attorneys general settlement (about $18.5 million).

Sources disagree on some details. Say which source you trust for each claim, and why.

Questions to think about as you research

  1. How did the attacker get in, and why did a vendor’s credentials work on Target’s network?
  2. Which network and security alerts fired, when, and who saw them? What would have made someone act?
  3. Where did stolen card data sit before it left, and what did the outbound traffic look like?
  4. Which single control would have changed the outcome the most: vendor access, segmentation, POS hardening, alert handling, or egress filtering?
  5. Compare with Mitnick: what kind of trust did each attacker abuse?
