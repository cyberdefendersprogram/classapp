The assignment

Research Kevin Mitnick and his attacks, including his confrontation with Tsutomu Shimomura. Write a case study that maps the intrusion to MITRE ATT&CK, says what a network sensor would have seen, and says what you would deploy today to stop or detect a similar attack.
