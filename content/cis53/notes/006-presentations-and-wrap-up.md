## Wrap-Up

- **How the course fits together:** collect (packets, flows, logs) → detect (rules, behavior, intelligence) → investigate (evidence, ATT&CK/ATLAS) → improve (tune and test). AI appears on all three sides: threats to AI, threats from AI, and detection with AI.
- **Where to go next:** TryHackMe SOC Level 1 and Level 2 paths, the AI Security path, and the detection engineering and threat hunting material from your readings. The optional books from the syllabus are good follow-ups.
- **Careers:** detection engineer, SOC analyst, threat hunter, incident responder, AI security engineer. Your Final write-ups and case study are portfolio pieces; strip any sensitive data before sharing.
